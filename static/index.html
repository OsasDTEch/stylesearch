Style Search

A working sketch of meaning based discovery for Madam, by Omons Wisdom. Ask the way you would ask a friend. Hybrid retrieval: dense embeddings + BM25 + a facet layer that actually knows hair (types, heat, manipulation, longevity), with every match explained.

low manipulation protective style for 4c hair, shoulder length, no heat something I can swim with on vacation that lasts 2 months polished style for a job interview, no heat wedding guest updo for 4b hair quick style for gym weeks
Demo dataset: 50 curated textured hair styles. The production version of this idea indexes the real catalog with image + text embeddings and a knowledge graph (styles, hair types, products, creators).
