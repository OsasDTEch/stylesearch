# 2 minute screen recording script (Loom or phone)

Record your screen with the demo open at http://localhost:8000. Talk naturally. No dashes needed in life either.

**0:00 to 0:15**
"Hey Caston, Wisdom here. Since I couldn't get into the app from Nigeria, I built you something instead. This is a working sketch of what meaning based discovery could feel like inside Madam."

**0:15 to 0:50**
Click the first example chip: *low manipulation protective style for 4c hair, shoulder length, no heat*.
"Watch what it understood: hair type 4c, no heat, low manipulation, protective, shoulder length. Every result is ranked by three signals together: semantic meaning, exact terms, and a layer that actually knows hair, so a silk press can never rank for a no heat query. And every match explains itself, see the green chips."

**0:50 to 1:20**
Type: *something I can swim with on vacation that lasts 2 months*.
"No hair words at all in that query. It still lands on Senegalese twists, knotless braids, faux locs, because it understands durations, swimming, occasions. This is the difference between search and a friend who knows hair."

"This is 50 curated styles and an afternoon of work. The production version indexes your real catalog with image embeddings, and connects styles, hair types, products and creators in a knowledge graph, the same architecture I used to lift answer accuracy 40% in my legal research engine. "Happy to walk through the code and where Daia fits whenever suits you. Talk soon."

## Tips
- Do ONE take after two practice runs, imperfect is fine, stiff is not
- Keep your face cam on if possible, founders buy people
